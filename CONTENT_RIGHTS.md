## Corrections and removal requests

For a missing credit, incorrect attribution or other public content correction, open a [correction issue](https://github.com/Kuldeep-psd/nid-giga-archive/issues/new?template=correction.yml) and identify the map and supporting source.

For a rights or privacy concern, contact [Kuldeep Singh](mailto:ks00347@gmail.com). Include the affected map URL, the concern, and the requested correction or removal. Do not post private supporting documents in a public issue.
